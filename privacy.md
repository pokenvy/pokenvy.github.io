# Pokenvy Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Pokenvy Discord bots ("Pokenvy", "we", "us") collect, why, and what you can do about it. By using Pokenvy, you agree to this policy.

## What we collect

**When you use a command**
- Your Discord user ID, username, and the server and channel you used the command in.
- What you asked for, such as the Pokémon set, item or file you submitted.
- Files you upload (for example Showdown set text files or PKM archives). These are processed to complete your request and are not kept afterwards.

**When you trade with a bot**
- Your in-game trainer name (OT), Trainer ID (TID) and Secret ID (SID), saved against your Discord user ID. We use this to give you Pokémon with your own trainer details and to show your profile card.
- The number of trades you have completed.
- Your Nintendo Switch online player ID and in-game name, recorded in trade logs.
- Copies of Pokémon you trade to the bot, which may be saved for checking legality and preventing abuse.

**Things you choose to give us**
- A profile quote, if you set one.
- A preferred trade code, if you set one.

**Moderation**
- If you are banned or flagged for abuse, your Discord user ID and/or Nintendo player ID are kept on a block list.

**Operational logs**
- The bots keep logs of their activity, which can include your Discord username, user ID and the commands you used.

We **do not** collect the content of normal chat messages. Pokenvy only sees what you send it through commands. We do not collect your email address, real name, location or payment card details.

## How we use it
- To run trades and complete the commands you request.
- To apply your trainer details to Pokémon and show your profile card.
- To keep the queue fair, prevent abuse and enforce bans.
- To fix problems with the bots.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as described below.

## Who can see it
- **Pokenvy staff** who run and moderate the bots.
- **Discord**, which handles all messages and commands. Discord's own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) applies.
- **Payment platforms**, if you donate. Donations are handled by a third-party platform, and we only see the information it shares with us (such as your name or username and the amount). Their privacy policy applies to your payment details.
- **Authorities**, if we are required to share information by law.

## How long we keep it
- Uploaded files are deleted once your request is processed.
- Trainer details, trade counts, trade codes and quotes are kept while Pokenvy is running, or until you ask us to delete them.
- Logs and saved Pokémon files are kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.
- Block list entries are kept as long as the ban is in place.

## Your choices
You can:
- Remove your saved trade code with `/deletetradecode`.
- Ask us to show you, or delete, the information we hold about you.

To make a request, join the Pokenvy Discord at [pokenvy.com](https://pokenvy.com) and open a ticket or message a staff member. Deleting your information resets your trade count and profile card. If you are banned, we will keep the minimum needed to enforce the ban.

## Children
Pokenvy is only for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement (13 in most countries, higher in some). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone below that age.

## Security
Information is stored on computers run by Pokenvy staff and is not publicly accessible. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be announced in the Pokenvy Discord.

## Contact
Questions? Join the Pokenvy Discord at [pokenvy.com](https://pokenvy.com) and open a ticket or message a staff member.

Pokenvy is a fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo, Game Freak, The Pokémon Company or Discord.
